## Item-name policy

- Fire, Ice, Electricity, Poison, and Magic are generic abilities and translate by meaning.
- Branded Sword and Rollerblade names remain unchanged in all Latin-script locales.
- Internal enum values, product IDs, save keys, and gameplay identifiers are never localized.
- The dormant future `zh-Hans` content retains its separate review in `ChineseProperNameQA.md` but is not part of this launch glossary.
