# Dormant/Future Simplified Chinese Proper-Name QA

`zh-Hans` is not part of the current launch locale set. This prior work is retained only so the locale can be re-enabled safely in a future release; it is not registered in Supported Locales or exposed in the production language menu.

Policy: these are phonetic brand renderings, not semantic translations. Pinyin is approximate and provided for review. Candidates were compared for sound, length, character associations, and consistency. None of the chosen compounds forms a common offensive or strongly negative phrase, but final native-speaker/brand approval is still recommended before store release.

## Candidate review notes

- Sound-first candidates using literal meaning characters (for example 血, 地狱, 冰霜, 龙, or 暗影) were rejected because they would semantically translate the brand.
- Shorter candidates were preferred only when they remained recognizable and did not introduce a stronger unintended phrase.
- UrbanRush and the longest multiword names carry Medium confidence and should receive native-speaker brand approval before final marketing lock.
- The internal IDs remain unchanged (`SwordId`, `RollerbladeId`, product IDs, and save data are unaffected).
